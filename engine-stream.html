AT A GLANCE

One workbench replaces chaotic vendor email chains.

  • ESN grouping — every scrapped part rolls up under its Engine Serial Number, so nothing gets lost across vendor threads.
  • Sticky filters — move between engines and vendors without losing your place in a dense table.
  • Status tags — Pending, Approved, Rejected, On-Hold, and Request Sent, color-coded for a one-glance read of what still needs action.
  • One-tap approve — replaces a full email reply-chain with a single click.
THE CHALLENGE

Scrap decisions were scattered across inboxes and spreadsheets.

WHAT CS/SALES WERE DEALING WITH

  • Constant context-switching: bouncing between Outlook, MRO databases, and tracking sheets just to confirm one scrap approval.
  • Spreadsheet duplication: data from upstream MRO systems re-typed into offline Excel trackers, one per Engine Serial Number.
  • Manual filing burden: every approved .msg email saved, renamed, and filed by hand for the audit record.
  • No visibility for customers: airlines had no window into pending scrap decisions, so status-check emails kept piling up.

WHAT THE APP DOES INSTEAD

  • One workbench: scrap data synced automatically, organized by ESN, with sticky filters and status tags.
  • Dedicated customer portal: airlines track engine progress and scrap decisions live, on their own.
  • One-click confirmation: an AI pre-fill paired with the source email snippet, confirmed in a single tap.
  • Automatic archiving: approved .msg files file themselves to cloud storage, no manual step required.

THE SOLUTION · APPROACH

A 3-phases rollout built to earn trust in AI.

Introducing AI into a high-stakes aerospace MRO workflow called for a gradual rollout, not a leap. Each phase hands the system a little more autonomy once the one before it has proven reliable.

PHASE 1

Human digitization. CS/Sales staff log part decisions directly in the web app and upload .msg files for the audit trail, while customers follow engine progress through a dedicated portal.

PHASE 2

AI verification (HITL). AI reads incoming email threads and pre-fills the likely decision. CS/Sales confirms each NLP read with a single approval click.

PHASE 3

Full AI autonomy. AI records customer decisions straight from email and archives the .msg files to cloud storage automatically, no human step required.

THE SOLUTION · FEATURE 01

Unified CS/Sales Scrap Workbench

UX rationale — a single-pane table over a multi-tab layout keeps every scrap part tied to its ESN. Sticky filters and color-coded status tags let staff move through dense part lists without losing their place.

  • ESN-grouped rows — consolidates scrapped-part data coming in from vendors via email and Excel, organized by engine.
  • Status tags — Pending, Approved, Rejected and On-Hold — no more juggling separate spreadsheets.
THE SOLUTION · FEATURE 02

Dedicated Airline Customer Portal

UX rationale — the client view is kept separate from internal vendor margins and private notes. Customers get full transparency into decisions, while inbound status emails and inbox back-and-forth drop off significantly.

  • Engine progress tracker — airline reps follow repair status without a single status-check email.
  • Scrap justification & photos — high-res inspection images sit next to each part's replacement rationale.
THE SOLUTION · FEATURE 03

Human-in-the-Loop AI Parsing Interface

UX rationale — pairing AI-suggested statuses with inline verification controls lets staff quickly clear pre-parsed approvals once verified against customer correspondence — streamlining manual review as a bridge toward full AI autonomy.

  • Verify AI Approval — CS/Sales confirms the AI's call with one click after checking the thread, eliminating manual data entry.
